I care more about how you feel in front of the camera than about perfect poses. Expect a walk, a lot of laughing, and photos that look like your trip actually felt.

FAQ

Good to know.

Anything else, just ask in your inquiry.

I've never been photographed. Is that okay?

Yes, most of my clients haven't. I'll show you simple poses and talk you through each spot.

Can you help with hanbok or wedding dress rental?

I can recommend rental shops near the shoot location and help with the booking in Korean.

What if it rains?

We reschedule to another day of your trip at no cost, or move to an indoor spot if you prefer.

How do I pay?

The deposit is paid by PayPal or Wise after your date is confirmed. The rest is paid on the shoot day.

Can I cancel?

Cancel 7 or more days before for a full deposit refund. After that, the deposit can move to a new date once.
